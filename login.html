How to Sign In to Your DhaniWin Account

  1. Open the official portal through the verified link above.
  2. Select the "Login" option.
  3. Enter your registered mobile phone number.
  4. Input your account password.
  5. Click "Login" to access your dashboard.

Troubleshooting Login Issues

  • Forgotten Password: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login window to request an SMS verification code and create a new password.
  • Network Latency: If the login screen fails to respond, toggle Airplane Mode or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile network.
  • Account Inquiries: Contact support on the official Telegram channel if your account requires verification.

Account Security Best Practices

  • Never divulge passwords or OTP codes to anyone.
  • Always verify the URL matches the official domain.
  • Avoid keeping saved passwords on public computers.
