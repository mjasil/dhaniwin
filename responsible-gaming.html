Safety & Responsible Use

Responsible Gaming

Responsible gaming information covering personal limits, account security, age and eligibility, local laws and avoiding chasing losses.

18+ Age Requirement: Online entertainment on linked services is intended exclusively for individuals 18 years of age or older. Verify that you meet the legal age requirements in your region before accessing any linked services.

Core Principles of Responsible Play

Gaming involves financial risk. If you choose to participate in a service, understand the rules, set personal limits and avoid spending money you cannot afford to lose.

  1. Set Monetary Limits: Establish a strict leisure budget and stick to it regardless of outcomes.
  2. Never Chase Losses: Treating past round results as debts to be recovered leads to compounding losses.
  3. Take Regular Breaks: Step away frequently to prevent fatigue and emotional decisions.
  4. Play for Leisure: Online gaming is entertainment, not an investment vehicle or financial solution.

Recognizing When to Pause

If gaming interferes with your day-to-day responsibilities, causes emotional distress, or exceeds your personal budget, seek assistance immediately and utilize voluntary cooling-off or account self-exclusion options.
